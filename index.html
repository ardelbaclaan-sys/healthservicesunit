ANNOUNCEMENT

All OLD and NEW students for Academic Year 2026–2027, particularly Grade 11 and Grade 12 Senior High School students, are required to accomplish and submit the Student Health and Wellness Record (SHWR).

Scan your Student ID or enter the Student ID number to verify SHWR submission.

1. QR CODE / BARCODE SCANNER
2. NFC SCANNER (SUPPORTED ANDROID DEVICES)
NFC requires Android Chrome, HTTPS, and a card containing a supported NDEF record with the Student ID.
3. MANUAL STUDENT ID ENTRY

IMPORTANT REMINDER

Use Google Chrome on Android for NFC scanning. Allow camera access when using the camera scanner.

NFC scanning works only if the card provides a supported NDEF record. Reading a card's MIFARE hardware UID is not supported by standard Web NFC.
